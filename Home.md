SigmaOS doesn't compete with generalist Linux. We specialize in:
1. **AI-Native Kernel**: Inference-aware scheduling and GPU acceleration.
2. **Sovereignty**: Post-quantum cryptography and isolated environments.
3. **Automation**: NixOS-inspired declarative configs.
