# 🌐 Social Scientist Profile

The Social Scientist Profile is designed for social research excellence, data analysis, and efficient academic workflows.

---

## 🛠️ Integrated Shards

- **SigmaSurvey**: Survey design and data collection.

- **SigmaAnalysis**: Social data analysis and visualization.

- **SigmaEthics**: Research ethics compliance and review.

- **SigmaPublication**: Publication management and collaboration.

---

## 🎨 Zenith Configuration

- **Theme**: Social Purple (Research Standard)

- **Layout**: Research Dashboard (Survey, Analysis, Ethics)

- **Personalization**: Custom survey tools and automated analysis.

---

## 🔒 Compliance & Integrity

SigmaOS ensures 100% social research data integrity through the **Research Sandbox**. All research data is logged in an immutable, PQC-signed audit trail for ethical compliance.
