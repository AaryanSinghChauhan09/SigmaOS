# Phase 19: Collective Consciousness & Cultural Sovereignty

SigmaOS Phase 19 marks the transition from computational sovereignty to **mind-adaptive sovereignty** — an OS that bridges collective human thought and enforces governance across civilizations.

## Track A: Collective-Conscious Shards

### Overview

Shards are modularised into three core layers:

- **Thought-Stream Acquisition** — captures neural signals, emotional valence, and resonance strength
- **Collective Cognition Engine** — merges individual thought-streams into a shared sovereign state
- **Shared-State Orchestration** — coordinates shard clusters based on group harmony index

### Key Modules

| Module | Path | Responsibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `collective_conscious.c` | `modules/core/kernel/` | Thought-stream acquisition & harmony evaluation |
| `neural_market.c` | `modules/core/kernel/` | Neural-adaptive shard trading |

### Harmony Algorithm

When `group_harmony_index > 80`, the engine automatically halves `shared_cognitive_load` — ensuring that high-resonance collectives use fewer resources, rewarding cooperation with performance.
