# SigmaOS Governance Charter

To establish institutional trust and manage a scaling community of contributors, SigmaOS is adopting a transparent governance model inspired by the success of the Debian and Fedora projects.

## 🏛️ The Sovereign Council
The Sovereign Council is the steering committee responsible for the long-term vision, architectural integrity, and community health of SigmaOS.
*   **Composition**: 5 elected members from the active contributor pool, plus the Project Lead.
*   **Term Length**: 12 months.
*   **Responsibilities**: Approving major architectural RFCs, managing the release schedule, and mediating community disputes.

## 🗳️ Meritocratic Decision Making
*   **Consensus Building**: Technical decisions are made through lazy consensus on public mailing lists and GitHub Issues.
*   **Voting**: If consensus cannot be reached, the Sovereign Council will vote. A simple majority resolves technical deadlocks.
*   **Contributor Status**: "Maintainer" status is granted based on consistent, high-quality code contributions over a 3-month period.

## 🤝 Community Standards
SigmaOS enforces a strict [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). We prioritize inclusivity, technical excellence, and constructive feedback. 
*   **Transparency**: All council meetings are logged and published in the GitHub Wiki.
*   **Sponsorship**: Corporate sponsorships are managed transparently. No single corporate entity may control more than 40% of the Sovereign Council.

## 📈 Path to Enterprise Trust
By adhering to this charter, SigmaOS aims to transition from a single-architect project to a globally trusted, decentralized operating system ecosystem.
