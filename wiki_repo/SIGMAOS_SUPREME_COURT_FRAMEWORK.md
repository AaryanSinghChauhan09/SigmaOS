# ⚖️ SigmaOS Supreme Court Framework

Article I — Jurisdiction
- Compliance Violations: Any breach of audit-ready standards (e.g., rollback failures, sandbox bypasses).
- Module Conflicts: Disputes between community-contributed compliance modules.
- User Rights Enforcement: Protection of rollback safety, transparency, and visual-first guarantees.

---

Article II — Structure
- Kernel Chamber → Oversees disputes related to kernel resilience and scheduler performance.
- Filesystem Chamber → Arbitrates rollback failures, journaling disputes, and distributed FS conflicts.
- Security Chamber → Rules on sandbox bypasses, firewall misconfigurations, and compliance breaches.
- Community Chamber → Reviews module contributions, ensuring compliance verification pipelines.

Article III — Procedures
- Audit Review → Every dispute begins with compliance log inspection.
- Rollback Test → Systems must demonstrate ≥ 99% rollback success before appeal.
- Sandbox Verification → GUI policies tested for enforcement accuracy.
- Community Arbitration → Conflicts between modules resolved via compliance-first pipelines.

---

Article IV — Enforcement
- Compliance Mandates → Violations trigger rollback enforcement or module suspension.
- User Remedies → Users guaranteed rollback recovery, transparency reports, and sandbox enforcement.
- Community Sanctions → Non-compliant modules are quarantined until verified.

---

Article V — Final Authority
The SigmaOS Supreme Court shall be the ultimate arbiter of compliance, resilience, and user rights, ensuring that:
- Linux’s fragmented governance and BSD’s conservative councils are rendered obsolete.
- SigmaOS remains the supreme operating system, trusted by industries where compliance and resilience are non-negotiable.

---

🌟 Strategic Outcome
This framework ensures SigmaOS is not just an OS but a governed ecosystem — with judicial oversight, compliance enforcement, and user rights enshrined.
Linux and BSD lack this structured governance, making SigmaOS the inevitable successor in regulated domains.
