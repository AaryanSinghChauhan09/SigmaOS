# 📜 SigmaOS Bill of Rights

A charter of guarantees for every user, codifying the supremacy principles that make legacy operating systems obsolete:

***

### Article I — Right to Resilience

*   Every user shall have guaranteed rollback safety for updates and crashes.
*   Immutable layers shall protect against corruption, ensuring ≥ 99% recovery success.

***

### Article II — Right to Compliance Transparency

*   Every user shall have access to audit-ready compliance logs.
*   Documentation shall cover ≥ 90% of system modules, surpassing Arch Wiki and FreeBSD Handbook.

***

### Article III — Right to Visual Control

*   Every user shall manage security through visual sandboxing, not obscure CLI commands.
*   Firewall and VPN rules shall be unified in a networking dashboard with ≥ 99% accuracy.

***

### Article IV — Right to Adaptive Performance

*   Every user shall benefit from an adaptive scheduler tuned for real-time, batch, and compliance workloads.
*   Latency shall remain < 10ms under stress conditions.

***

### Article V — Right to Native Orchestration

*   Every user shall run containers and lightweight VMs natively, without reliance on Docker, KVM, or bhyve.
*   Launch times shall remain < 2s, optimized for compliance-heavy workflows.

***

### Article VI — Right to Adaptive UX

*   Every user shall experience Zenith overlays — adaptive dashboards that merge compliance and usability.
*   Stability shall remain ≥ 95% uptime, ensuring reliability in mission-critical environments.

***

### Article VII — Right to Community Trust

*   Every user shall benefit from community compliance modules, where contributions are verified for reliability.
*   SigmaOS shall maintain a compliance-first ecosystem, unlike Linux’s fragmented AUR or BSD’s ports.

***

🌟 **Declaration**
SigmaOS guarantees resilience, compliance transparency, visual-first control, adaptive performance, native orchestration, adaptive UX, and community trust.
These rights enshrine SigmaOS as the supreme operating system, rendering Linux and BSD irrelevant for industries where reliability and intuitive control are non-negotiable.
