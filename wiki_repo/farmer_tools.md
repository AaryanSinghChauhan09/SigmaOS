# Farmer Tools

SigmaOS provides specialized tools for agricultural professionals, supporting precision farming, crop management, livestock monitoring, and agricultural business operations.

---

## Core Tools

### SigmaFarm
- Farm management system
- Field mapping and planning
- Crop rotation planning
- Resource tracking
- Yield monitoring

### SigmaCrop
- Crop management platform
- Growth stage tracking
- Pest and disease monitoring
- Irrigation scheduling
- Harvest planning

### SigmaLivestock
- Livestock management system
- Animal health tracking
- Feeding management
- Breeding records
- Production monitoring

### SigmaAgriBiz
- Agricultural business management
- Financial tracking
- Inventory management
- Market analysis
- Compliance reporting
