# Hardware Compatibility Matrix

Supported architectures, systems, and peripherals for SigmaOS.

## Architectures
- **x86_64**: Fully supported (Multicore, AVX-512, UEFI).
- **ARM64 / RISC-V**: Experimental.

## Devices & Controllers
- **USB**: EHCI (USB 2.0) and xHCI (USB 3.0).
- **Storage**: NVMe (PCI Express) and AHCI (SATA).
- **Network**: Intel e1000, Realtek RTL8139, and virtio-net.\n
