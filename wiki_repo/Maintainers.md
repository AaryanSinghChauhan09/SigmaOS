## Becoming a Maintainer

To become a maintainer for a subsystem:

1. Demonstrate consistent, high-quality contributions to that subsystem
2. Show understanding of the architecture and design principles
3. Participate in code reviews for that subsystem
4. Be responsive to issues and PRs
5. Follow the [Maintenance Policy](Maintenance-Policy.md)

Maintainers are expected to:
- Review and merge PRs for their subsystem within 7 days
- Respond to issues tagged with their subsystem
- Participate in architectural discussions
- Mentor new contributors
- Keep subsystem documentation up to date
