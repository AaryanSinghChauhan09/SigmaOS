## 🚀 Why SigmaOS Wins


1.  **Unified Kernel**: One codebase targets Bare-Metal, VM, and Browser (Chromium) simultaneously via the **UAL**.
2.  **Zero-Dependency**: No reliance on `glibc`, `systemd`, or legacy X11/Wayland bloat.
3.  **Atomic Modularity**: Switch from a Kali-style security auditor to an Alpine-style minimal kernel with a single `s-cli profile` command.
4.  **Silicon Sovereignty**: Direct interaction with hardware primitives, bypassing the "OS-as-a-guest" limitations of legacy distributions.
