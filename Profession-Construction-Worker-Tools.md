# 👷 Construction Worker Profile

The Construction Worker Profile is designed for construction excellence, site management, and efficient construction workflows.

---

## 🛠️ Integrated Shards

- **SigmaSite**: Site management and safety tracking.

- **SigmaTask**: Task assignment and progress tracking.

- **SigmaMaterial**: Material inventory and ordering.

- **SigmaSafety**: Safety compliance and incident reporting.

---

## 🎨 Zenith Configuration

- **Theme**: Safety Orange (Construction Standard)

- **Layout**: Construction Dashboard (Site, Task, Material)

- **Personalization: Custom checklists and automated alerts.

---

## 🔒 Compliance & Integrity

SigmaOS ensures 100% construction data integrity through the **Construction Sandbox**. All site activities are logged in an immutable, PQC-signed audit trail for safety compliance.
