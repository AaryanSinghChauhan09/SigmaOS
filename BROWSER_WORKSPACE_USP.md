# SigmaOS: The Workspace-as-a-Browser

SigmaOS transcends the traditional web browser. By marrying operating system abstractions with browser agility, SigmaOS is positioned as a **"Browser that thinks in tasks, not tabs."**

## Core Unique Selling Points (USPs)

### 1. 🗂️ Smart Tab Workflows
Instead of messy rows of disconnected tabs, SigmaOS automatically groups tabs by project, context, or domain (e.g., Coding Docs, GitHub Repositories, Sololearn Tutorials).

### 2. ✅ Task-Linked Tabs
Attach tabs directly to your to-do items or project tasks. When you open a task, its specific environment of references, tools, and documentation loads instantly.

### 3. 🧠 Adaptive Tab Memory
SigmaOS remembers the state of your work. Tabs reopen with their exact scroll position, internal highlights, and attached context notes perfectly preserved.

### 4. 🎓 Learning Integration
SigmaOS natively detects when you are on learning platforms (like Sololearn, LeetCode, or FreeCodeCamp) and auto-logs your progress into a centralized Learning Dashboard.

### 5. 🤝 Built-in Collaboration
Share entire "workspaces" or tab groups with your teammates. Shared workspaces include your localized annotations, ensuring your team sees exactly what you see.

### 6. 🧑‍💻 Developer-Oriented Tools
* **GitHub Integration:** Inline repository previews, issue tracking, and PR commenting directly within the Sovereign Omnibox.
* **Code Snippet Manager:** Highlight text on any documentation site and instantly save it to a globally searchable snippet library.
* **API Playground:** A built-in, lightweight REST client allowing developers to test endpoints without launching Postman or Insomnia.

### 7. 📚 Legal & Academic Mode
Designed for intense research, this mode auto-organizes references, scrapes citations, and formats notes automatically when browsing academic journals or video lectures.

### 8. 🔄 Offline Continuity
A true OS doesn't die when the WiFi drops. Offline Continuity allows you to edit tabs, write annotations, and manage tasks locally, syncing automatically when connectivity is restored.
