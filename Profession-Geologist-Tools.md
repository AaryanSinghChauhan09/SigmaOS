# 🌋 Geologist Profile

The Geologist Profile is designed for geological excellence, field research, and efficient earth science workflows.

---

## 🛠️ Integrated Shards

- **SigmaField**: Field research management and sample collection.

- **SigmaAnalysis**: Geological analysis and mapping tools.

- **SigmaSeismic**: Seismic data processing and monitoring.

- **SigmaResearch**: Research collaboration and publication.

---

## 🎨 Zenith Configuration

- **Theme**: Earth Brown (Geology Standard)

- **Personalization**: Custom mapping tools and automated alerts.

## 🔒 Compliance & Integrity

SigmaOS ensures 100% geological data integrity through the **Research Sandbox**. All field data is logged in an immutable, PQC-signed audit trail for scientific integrity.
