
# SigmaOS Community & Contribution


SigmaOS is more than an operating system; it is a **Sovereign Entity** built by a global community of low-level enthusiasts and industrial pioneers.


## How to Contribute



### 1. Lattice Sharding

Help us expand the 33-suite lattice by contributing new shards. Follow the [Contributor Guide](ContributorGuide.md) for pure silicon standards.


### 2. UI/UX Refinement

Improve the **Zenith Dashboard** by refining the **Sovereign UI Toolkit** or adding new observability modules.


### 3. Driver Development

Provide reference implementations for specialized hardware using our **Modular Driver** paradigm.


## Communication Channels

- **GitHub Discussions**: For architectural proposals and lattice mapping.
- **Sovereign Shell**: Use `sigma chat` within the Zenith Terminal to interact with other contributors in real-time.


## Code of Conduct

We maintain a culture of **Silicon Excellence**. All contributions must be freestanding, documented, and architecturally verified.
