# Quarterly Review Runbook

## Purpose
Reassess architecture, gaps, security, and roadmap. Archive stale research older than two quarters.

## Workflow
1. Re-evaluate all component maturity levels in `CAPABILITY_MATRIX.toml` and `FEATURE_STATUS.toml`.
2. Review security boundaries and threat models.
3. Update roadmap and backlog priorities (P0, P1, P2).
4. Archive stale research notes older than two quarters in `research/archive/`.
5. Publish quarterly report in `research/reports/quarterly/`.
