# SigmaOS GUI Development Plan

## Design and Acceptance Rules
Authoritative rules and quality standards for GUI components.

## Proposed 19-PR Sequence Roadmap
19 targeted PRs from Audit Baseline to Hardware and Release Readiness.

## Periodic Maintenance PR Cadences
Weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, and quarterly cadences for GUI component maintenance.

## Component Rules
Strict isolation, IPC boundaries, and zero unbounded recursion.

## PR Review and Merge Gates
Automated checks, test coverage enforcement, and review signoffs.

## Rollout and Exit Criteria
Criteria required for promotion from prototype to production supported state.
