# SigmaOS GUI Development Plan

## Design and Acceptance Rules
Strict headless/nested tests, clean rollback, and security sandboxing.

## Proposed 19-PR Sequence Roadmap
19-PR roadmap covering baseline audit to hardware release readiness.

## Periodic Maintenance PR Cadences
Weekly and monthly cadences for regression sweeps and compositor hardening.

## Component Rules
Strict component isolation for wayland, input, rendering, and accessibility.

## PR Review and Merge Gates
Automated test gates and manual visual verification.

## Rollout and Exit Criteria
Zero headless crashes, full feature parity, and accessibility certification.
