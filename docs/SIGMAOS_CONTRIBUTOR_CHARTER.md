# 📜 SigmaOS Contributor Charter

## 🔹 Purpose
The SigmaOS Contributor Charter establishes the principles, responsibilities, and processes for contributing to the SigmaOS ecosystem. It ensures that development remains sovereign, secure, and collaborative.

---

## 🧩 Core Principles
- **Sovereignty** → All contributions must uphold independence from proprietary blobs and closed ecosystems.
- **Clarity** → Code, documentation, and governance must be transparent and declarative.
- **Resilience** → Contributions should strengthen rollback, recovery, and cluster-native features.
- **Security** → Rust safety, sandboxing, and cryptographic guarantees are non-negotiable.

---

## 🔹 Contributor Roles
- **Core developers** → Maintain kernel, init system, package manager, networking, and filesystem shards.
- **Module maintainers** → Oversee specific shards (drivers, utilities, advanced features).
- **Community contributors** → Submit patches, documentation, translations, and accessibility improvements.
- **Institutional partners** → Universities, labs, and governments testing sovereignty features.

---

## 🔹 Contribution Process
1. **Proposal** → Submit a declarative manifest of changes.
2. **Review** → Peer review by shard maintainers with double maintainer signoff requirement.
3. **Testing** → Automated checks for Rust safety, reproducibility, and sovereignty compliance (`./run_sigma_tests.sh`).
4. **Merge** → Approved contributions integrated into the shard ecosystem.
5. **Recognition** → Contributors listed in the SigmaOS Hall of Fame.

---

## 🔹 Governance
- **Stewardship Council** → Elected maintainers oversee roadmap alignment.
- **Consensus model** → Major decisions require community voting and double maintainer code review.
- **Transparency reports** → Quarterly updates on progress, telemetry, and governance.

---

## 🔹 Community Engagement
- **Hackathons & sprints** → Regular events to attract new talent.
- **Documentation wiki** → Living knowledge base for onboarding.
- **Contributor badges** → Recognition for milestones.
- **Mentorship program** → Pair new contributors with experienced maintainers.

---

## 🌍 Outcome
The Contributor Charter ensures SigmaOS grows as a sovereign, collaborative ecosystem. It balances innovation with governance, empowering individuals and institutions to build the post-Linux sovereign OS together.
