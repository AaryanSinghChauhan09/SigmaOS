## 🏗️ 2. Architectural Analysis: Why It's Different

### 🛰️ The No-Server Advantage
Mainstream apps like WhatsApp and Signal require a central server to route messages. If the server goes down (or is blocked), communication stops.
- **BitChat/SigmaMesh**: Uses **Bluetooth Mesh** to hop messages between local devices until they reach the destination or an internet relay.

### 🔐 Identity Sovereignty
- **Traditional**: Your identity is tied to a SIM card or phone number—controlled by a telco or government.
- **Sovereign**: Your identity is a **Private Key** generated locally on your device. You own your name; no central authority can "suspend" your account.

### 🤖 OS-Native Integration (SigmaOS USP)
While BitChat is currently a standalone app, SigmaOS integrates this protocol directly into the **Kernel Syscall Map**.
- **Unified Action**: A message received via SovereignMesh can trigger a kernel-level hardware shutdown or a workstation profile switch instantly.
