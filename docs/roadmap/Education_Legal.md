# Education & Legal Integration

## Educational Suite
SigmaOS aims to bridge the digital divide by shipping pre-configured educational tools:
- **Math & Science:** GeoGebra, Scilab, Octave.
- **E-Learning:** Local offline Moodle instances, flashcard frameworks, and practice test modules.

## Legal Tools & Compliance
- Integrated workflows for Indian Kanoon and SCC Online APIs.
- Deep compliance with Indian DPDP (Digital Personal Data Protection) Act regarding data locality and offline-first workflows.
