# Design & Creativity Tools

To support creators without requiring them to navigate complex dependency hells, SigmaOS provides a rich, atomic suite of design tools.

## Graphics & Video
- **Vector & Raster:** Inkscape (Vector), Krita (Raster), Blender (3D).
- **UI/UX Design:** Penpot (Open-source Figma alternative).
- **Video Editing:** Kdenlive, Shotcut.
- **Audio Production:** LMMS, Ardour.

All media applications are routed through the native Wayland Zenith compositor for zero-latency rendering and accurate color calibration profiles.
