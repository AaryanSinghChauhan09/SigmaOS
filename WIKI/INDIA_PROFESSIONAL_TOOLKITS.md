# 🇮🇳 SigmaOS Indian Professional Toolkits Map

This document outlines the profession-specific software toolkits integrated into SigmaOS. Inspired by Linux and BSD modularity, these toolkits are tailored for Indian legal, healthcare, education, corporate, IT, agricultural, creative, and digital forensics domains.

---

## ⚖️ 1. Legal & Judicial Domain

- **`SigmaLaw`**: Case law search, citation indexing, and court cause-list tracking.
- **`SigmaNotary`**: Digital signature (eSign) and e-stamp verification for affidavits and contracts.
- **`SigmaCourt`**: Automated legal filing preparation and compliance dashboards.

---

## 🏥 2. Healthcare & Medical Domain

- **`SigmaMed`**: Patient Electronic Health Records (EHR) management with ABDM (Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission) compliance.
- **`SigmaPharma`**: Drug inventory tracking, batch validation, and e-prescription verification.
- **`SigmaTeleHealth`**: End-to-end encrypted video consultations with secure medical record exchange.

---

## 📚 3. Education & Academia Domain

- **`SigmaEdu`**: Learning Management System (LMS) with modular course dashboards.
- **`SigmaExam`**: Automated exam generation, proctoring, and grading analysis.
- **`SigmaResearch`**: Plagiarism detection, citation management, and collaborative notebook environment.

---

## 💼 4. Corporate & Business Domain

- **`SigmaBiz`**: ERP-style accounting, GST invoice generation, and inventory management.
- **`SigmaPayroll`**: Automated payroll calculations with EPF, ESI, and TDS compliance dashboards.
- **`SigmaAudit`**: Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) monitoring suite.

---

## 🛠️ 5. Engineering, IT & Cybersecurity Domain

- **`SigmaDev`**: Developer IDE with cross-compilation support for Rust, Zig, Nim, C, and Python.
- **`SigmaInfra`**: Container, VM, and microVM orchestration engine.
- **`SigmaCyber`**: Security audit toolkit with intrusion detection, packet analysis, and vulnerability scanning.

---

## 🌾 6. Agriculture & Rural Development Domain

- **`SigmaAgri`**: Crop health monitoring, soil analytics, and satellite weather dashboards.
- **`SigmaMarket`**: Real-time mandi price tracking and e-NAM market integration.
- **`SigmaSupply`**: Cold-chain logistics and agricultural supply chain tracking.

---

## 🎨 7. Creative & Media Domain

- **`SigmaStudio`**: Non-linear multi-track audio and video editing suite with GPU acceleration.
- **`SigmaPublish`**: Desktop publishing workflow for books, journals, and blogs.
- **`SigmaDesign`**: Vector graphics editor and AR/VR prototyping environment.

---

## 🔍 8. Digital Forensics & Law Enforcement Domain

- **`SovereignForensicsEngine`**: Memory dump acquisition, disk file carving, and SHA-256 evidence chain-of-custody logging.
- **`SigmaForensicWriteBlocker`**: Software-enforced read-only storage device filter preventing evidence tampering.
- **`SigmaTimelineCorrelator`**: Multi-source log correlation and e-discovery report generator.
