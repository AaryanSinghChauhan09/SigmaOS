# 📜 The Sovereign Lattice: An Architectural Manifesto for SigmaOS

## 🔳 The End of the Monolith
For decades, the computing industry has been shackled by monolithic technical debt. Operating systems like Linux and Windows, while powerful, have reached their entropy limits. They are fragile, bloated, and inherently insecure at the silicon level.

## ⚙️ The Shard Paradigm
SigmaOS introduces the **Sovereign Lattice (S33)**. Every system component—from a Wi-Fi driver to the memory allocator—is a self-contained, independent **Shard**.
- **Atomicity**: Shards are hot-loadable and hot-swappable.
- **Zero-Trust**: Shards are cryptographically namespaced.
- **Lego-like Orchestration**: The architect can synthesize a custom lattice for any device (Embedded, IoT, Supercomputer) by selecting specific suites.

## 🛡️ Zero-Day Sovereignty
By enforcing strict **S08 Compliance** and **S30 Supremacy Signatures**, the lattice remains immutable against upstream supply-chain attacks. SigmaOS does not "patch" vulnerabilities; it re-synthesizes the affected shard in real-time.
