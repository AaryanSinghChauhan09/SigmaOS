💰 Income Tax Calculator FY 2024–25
Under Section 115BAC, Income Tax Act, 1961 (New Tax Regime): 0–3L @0%, 3–7L @5%, 7–10L @10%, 10–12L @15%, 12–15L @20%, above 15L @30%. Standard deduction ₹75,000 u/s 16(ia). Rebate u/s 87A for income ≤ ₹7L.
🧾 GST Calculator
Under Central Goods and Services Tax (CGST) Act, 2017 (Section 9): CGST = SGST = GST Rate ÷ 2 (for intra-state supply). For inter-state supply, full IGST applies under Section 5 of the IGST Act, 2017.
📋 TDS Calculator
As per Income Tax Act, 1961: Sec 192 (Salary), Sec 194C (Contractor), Sec 194J (Professional fees), Sec 194I (Rent), Sec 194A (Interest), Sec 194H (Commission). Sec 206AA demands 20% or higher rate if PAN is not furnished.
🏦 Gratuity Calculator
Under Section 4(2) of the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972: Salary × 15/26 × Completed years of service. Maximum tax-free exemption limit is ₹20,00,000 as per Section 10(10) of the Income Tax Act, 1961.
🏛️ Court Fee Calculator
As per the Court-fees Act, 1870 (Section 7 & Schedule I/II). Court fees vary across State amendments. This uses standard general estimates; always verify with respective High Court rules.
📜 Stamp Duty Calculator
As per the Indian Stamp Act, 1899 (and respective State amendments). Rates vary strictly by state, nature of document, and municipal limits. Female buyer concessions apply in several states (e.g., Delhi, Haryana).
