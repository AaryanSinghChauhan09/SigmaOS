MANDATORY FILING Sec 139, Income Tax Act

Zero-Tax Liability ≠ Zero Filing

Even if your calculated tax is mathematically ₹0, if your gross salary exceeds ₹2.5 Lakhs (Old) or ₹3 Lakhs (New), you MUST file an ITR. Failing to do so allows the state to freeze accounts via Sec 276CC.

⚖️ Latest Court Update: ITAT mandates that Claiming Refunds (TDS) strictly requires ITR filing within the assessment year deadline.
DATA PRIVACY SHIELD DPDP Act, 2023

Absolute Consent Overreach

Corporations cannot legally force you to consent to promotional marketing to use a basic service. The 'Notice And Consent' mechanism gives you the sovereign right to revoke data access instantly.

⚖️ Case Law Benchmark: Justice K.S. Puttaswamy v. UoI (SC) strictly categorizes informational privacy as an intrinsic subset of Article 21 (Right to Life).
E-DAAKHIL POWERS Consumer Protection Act, 2019

Direct Corporate Accountability

If you buy a defective laptop or face food poisoning, you possess a **2-Year Window** to file a complaint digitally on E-Daakhil. No lawyer is required for claims under ₹5 Lakhs.

⚖️ Core Paradigm: Product Liability (Sec 84) explicitly makes the E-commerce platform liable alongside the manufacturer for defective deliveries.
PRESUMPTIVE SHIELD Sec 44ADA, IT Act

The 50% Expense Extrapolation

Software developers, UI designers, and consultants earning under ₹75 Lakhs/year can legally declare 50% of their income as "Expenses" without maintaining a single audit book or receipt.

⚖️ Finance Act Update: The limit was raised from ₹50L to ₹75L explicitly for professionals receiving 95% of payments digitally.
GST REGISTRATION TRIGGER Sec 22, CGST Act

The 20 Lakh Service Threshold

If you provide software services within India, GST registration is absolutely mandatory the second your turnover crosses ₹20 Lakhs. **However**, Export of Services (to US/Europe) are "Zero-Rated" if an LUT is filed.

⚖️ Procedural Matrix: Filing a Letter of Undertaking (LUT) before March 31st ensures your foreign client invoices remain tax-free without blocking capital.
MSME 45-DAY MANDATE Sec 43B(h), IT Act

Giant Corporations Must Pay You

If your startup is Udyam Registered (Micro/Small), you must be paid within 45 days. If a large corporation defaults, they **cannot claim tax deductions** on that expense, forcing them to pay you to save their own taxes.

⚖️ Enforcement Heuristic: The Samadhaan Portal allows startups to trigger MSME facilitation councils directly, bypassing civil courts entirely for delayed payments.
BOARD MEETING ENFORCEMENT Sec 173, Companies Act

The 120-Day Max Gap

Running a Private Limited Company requires at least 4 Board Meetings a year. The gap between two meetings must mathematically **never exceed 120 days**. (Special exception exists for 'Small Companies' - only 2 meetings required).

⚖️ Compliance Penalty: ROC (Registrar of Companies) imposes automatic algorithmic penalties for missing DIR-12 or failing to properly minute the meetings within 30 days.
