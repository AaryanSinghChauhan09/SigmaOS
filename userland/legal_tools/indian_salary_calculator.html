📖 Explanations

💰 CTC vs Gross vs Net Take-Home

CTC = Total cost to employer (includes your salary + employer's PF + bonus etc.)
Gross Salary = Your monthly salary before deductions
Net / Take-Home = Money actually credited to your bank account after all cuts

⚖️ New 50% Wage Rule (Nov 2025)

India's new Labour Codes say your Basic + DA must be ≥ 50% of total salary. If your company was keeping Basic low (e.g., 30%) to reduce PF, they must now restructure. This means your PF, gratuity & bonus will go up — but take-home may slightly reduce.

🏦 What is PF / EPF?

Employee Provident Fund — you save 12% of Basic+DA every month, and your employer adds another 12%. This money earns 8.25% interest (tax-free!) and you get it all when you retire or leave the job. It's your retirement savings.

🎖️ What is Gratuity?

A thank-you payment from your employer after long service. Formula: Basic+DA × 15/26 × Years Served. Earlier you needed 5 years — now Fixed-Term employees get it after just 1 year (new Labour Code, effective Nov 2025).
