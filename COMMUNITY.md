# Community Engagement

Welcome to the SigmaOS Community! We are building a post-POSIX, zero-abstraction OS, and we need your help to shape the future of computing.

## Communication Channels

### 1. GitHub Discussions (Asynchronous)
For architectural proposals, Q&A, and showcasing what you've built.
👉 [Join GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/AaryanSinghChauhan09/SigmaOS/discussions)

### 2. Matrix Space (Real-Time Chat)
Our primary real-time chat for kernel hacking, userland app development, and general hangout.
👉 `#sigmaos:matrix.org`

### 3. Mailing Lists
For formal patch submissions and security announcements.
- `kernel-dev@sigmaos.local`
- `security-announce@sigmaos.local`

## Contributing
Ready to write some code? 
1. Check out our [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) guide.
2. Sign the [DCO.md](DCO.md).
3. Find an issue labeled `good first issue` on our tracker.

## Governance
We follow a benevolent consensus model described in our [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md). We are committed to a welcoming environment per our [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
