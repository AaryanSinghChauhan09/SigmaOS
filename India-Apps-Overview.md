# India Professional Apps Overview

SigmaOS supports sovereign, localized integrations tailored for professional workloads in India:

## 1. eCourts Deep Integration (`sigma-judicial`)
- Connects directly with Indian eCourts systems.
- Integrates case retrieval, case status tracking, and automated legal filings.

## 2. MSME Platform (`sigma-msme`)
- Seamless integrations for Udyam registration, GeM (Government e-Marketplace), TReDS (Trade Receivables Discounting System), and OCEN (Open Credit Enablement Network).

## 3. Voter Services (`sigma-elections`)
- Built-in portal for EPIC validation, polling station finders, and candidate affidavit inspection.

## 4. AYUSH Healthcare (`sigma-ayush`)
- Digital integration for AYUSH practitioner registry, Ayurvedic Formulary of India (AFI), Panchakarma center registries, and NABH compliance tools.

## 5. Environmental Compliance (`sigma-climate`)
- Connectivity to CPCB (Central Pollution Control Board) portals, Environmental Clearance monitoring, Carbon Markets, ESG reporting, and AQI analytics.

## 6. Broadcast & Press Compliance (`sigma-media`)
- Tools for MIB (Ministry of Information and Broadcasting) registration, OTT IT Rules compliance, Press Registrar tracking, and TRAI DAS auditing.

## 7. Water Resource Management (`sigma-water`)
- Automated integrations with CWC (Central Water Commission), JJM (Jal Jeevan Mission), and real-time Flood Warning systems.

## 8. Correctional Facility Management (`sigma-prison`)
- Integrates with ePrisons, BNSS (Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita) timelines, bail processing, and inmate rehabilitation tracking.

## 9. Customs & Logistics (`sigma-port`)
- Direct ICEGATE, PCS1x, Bill of Lading, FASTag, and EXIM Bank API orchestration.

## 10. Land Records & Survey (`sigma-land`)
- Automated synchronization with DILRMP land maps, mutations, Bhu-Naksha, and SVAMITVA records.

## 11. PM WANI Wi-Fi (`sigma-wani`)
- Implements TRAI PDO registry, UPI payments, and PM-WANI public hotspot configuration.

## 12. DigiYatra Travel (`sigma-digiyatra`)
- Facilitates biometric airport and railway credentials verification, face enrollment, and boarding.

## 13. e-Shram Worker Registration (`sigma-eshram`)
- Unorganised worker scheme profiles, gig worker compliance, and BoCW registrations.

## 14. India Post Banking (`sigma-ippb`)
- Doorstep banking tracking, AePS (Aadhaar Enabled Payment System) integration, and savings scheme synchronization.

## 15. IRCTC Integration (`sigma-irctc`)
- Deep train seat map, PNR, running status lookup, Tatkal, and UTS booking automation.

## 16. COWIN / U-WIN Immunisation (`sigma-cowin`)
- Vaccine records lookup, U-WIN pediatric immunization scheduler, and AEFI tracking.

## 17. Census & NPR (`sigma-census`)
- Household surveys, NPR population statistics, and coverage maps.
